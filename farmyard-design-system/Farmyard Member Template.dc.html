03 · Look — locked

Line & shade

Heavy black ink outline, thicker on the silhouette. Flat cel shading, two tones per colour, hard shadow edges. Sticker / skate-graphic finish, slight grain.

Pose & attitude

Three-quarter view, bust to waist, filling the disc. Cocky, mid-grin or mid-scowl, one eyebrow up. Each character owns one prop or garment.

Palette

Pink and cyan on every character. Tiger-eye orange or cream as the body colour. Nothing else.

Cartoon mascot of a [ANIMAL], [ATTITUDE], three-quarter view from the waist up, holding/wearing [ONE PROP OR GARMENT]. Style: bold black ink outlines, flat cel shading with hard shadow edges, sticker / skate-graphic finish, slight print grain. Exactly one element ([PROP]) breaks past the ring by no more than 80px. Everything outside the disc and glow is transparent. No text, no extra background, no gradient fill in the disc.

Breathing glow only at hero and card sizes. 96px is the smallest the full-colour art goes. From 75px down each character switches to its face mark — a vector line drawing of the head — inside a CSS ring (1.5px teal, 6px glow) drawn by the page — ring and line share one colour.

Face mark spec

SVG, 256 × 256 viewBox, transparent, no ring (the page draws it). Line-trace of the head from the full art — the ink outlines only, no fill, no shading, fur and background dropped, faded to nothing at the edge of the circle. Same three-quarter angle. Hairline weight (≈1px on screen). One colour: neon teal #5fd3cf with a 4px teal drop-shadow glow. Nothing in pink below 96px.

Face mark prompt
